Our Story

Regal Gold was born in Jeddah with one clear idea — to make jewellery buying feel personal, honest, and easy.

Our showroom brings together rich traditional designs, modern diamond jewellery, and pure gold for gifting, wearing, and saving.

From 22K gold jewellery and 18K diamond pieces to 24K coins and bars, every product is chosen with focus on design, purity, fair pricing, and customer trust.

Regal Gold is made for families, brides, young women, working professionals, and gold lovers who want jewellery that feels right the moment they see it.

Why We Do What We Do

Our Mission: To craft and curate jewellery that honours heritage, celebrates the present, and is worthy of being passed on — making every customer feel seen, valued, and cared for, every time they step into our world.
